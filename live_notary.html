How a signing runs

Six steps, in the same order every time, so everyone at the closing table knows what happens and when.

01

Package handoff

Send the closing package and signing instructions ahead of the appointment, or tell me where the file is queued.

02

Time and place

The signing is booked around the closing table — your office, the borrower's location, or ours.

03

Identity verification

Borrower identification is checked and the notarial record is completed for every signature.

04

Document walkthrough

Each signer is taken through every page — notes, deeds, closing disclosures, TRID disclosures — before anything is signed.

05

Notarization

Signatures and initials are witnessed and notarized, and seals are applied to the executed set.

06

File returned

The signed, notarized package comes back as one complete set, ready to fund.
